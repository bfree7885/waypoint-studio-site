## Mission

*Capture what you find. Learn why it matters.*

---

## Primary products

### Dashboard (Free)

Outdoor mission control for planning today.

Helps visitors understand daily conditions and what they mean — weather, photography windows, sun & moon, AQI, rivers, alerts, nearby volunteer opportunities, and related articles — with **Waypoint’s Take** interpreting why it matters.

**Dashboard 3.0** (`/dashboard/`): thirteen customizable category widgets, local layout preferences, kiosk mode, and Open-Meteo-backed conditions with demo fallback. See [DASHBOARD-3.md](./DASHBOARD-3.md).

### Scenes (Flagship)

Outdoor photography as a serious craft: living landscape experiences, Photo Coach, portfolio building, AI-assisted culling, collections, Hidden Landscapes, and outdoor discovery.

Public route today: `/waypoint-scenes/`. Nav label: **Scenes**.

### Sheds (Flagship)

A dedicated shed hunting platform — equal in stature to Scenes. Map-first habitat intelligence, find logging, heat maps, analytics, and seasonal learning. Ethical field practice; demo zones only in the current prototype.

### Volunteer (Free)

Discover meaningful outdoor stewardship: trail work, conservation, citizen science, animal care, and community help. Anchored by the question *What good can I do today?*

---

## Platform layer (not a separate product)

### Articles

Educational resource that supports every flagship experience. Categories: Photography, Shed Hunting, Mushrooms, Wildlife, Conservation, Outdoor Skills, Science, Seasonal Guides.

Public route: `/education/` (Articles). Species Learn pages under ForageCast remain linked from Articles.

### Waypoint’s Take

Shared interpretation pattern. Turns observations into calm, educational insight. Never clickbait. Future AI can deepen Takes without redesigning the UI contract.
