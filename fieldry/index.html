Fieldry

A real-world nature journal for noticing more.

Fieldry is a future nature journaling app inspired by field guides, observation, ecology, and slow attention to the living world.

Subscription model coming soon.

Features

Personal field journal

A quiet space to record what you notice — dates, places, and impressions.

Species observations

Log plants, animals, and fungi with context rather than gamified checklists.

Place-based discoveries

Connect observations to specific locations and return to them over seasons.

Seasonal collections

Organize finds by time of year — building a personal natural history over time.

Kid-friendly nature noticing

Simple prompts and collections designed for families learning together outdoors.

Slow, non-addictive design

No streaks, no likes, no pressure — built for attention, not engagement metrics.
