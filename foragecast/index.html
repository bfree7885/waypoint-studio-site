What it does

ForageCast combines terrain clues, recent weather, soil temperature proxies, elevation, slope and aspect, forest edge context, and seasonal timing to estimate when conditions may be improving for target species — not where to extract them.

The system is built for habitat awareness and ethical field decision-making. Predictions describe environmental readiness, not guaranteed finds.

Ethical foraging rules

  • Know property boundaries before you enter any land.
  • Follow state and local regulations, permits, and seasonal restrictions.
  • Harvest lightly — leave enough for regeneration and wildlife.
  • Leave habitat intact. Avoid trampling understory and disturbing soils.
  • Do not rely on app predictions as permission to enter land.
  • Learn identification from trusted sources before consuming anything.

Research & data vision

ForageCast is intended to become a structured field-observation and environmental modeling platform. Over time, user observations — paired with terrain, weather, and habitat GIS layers — will build toward repeatable seasonal models grounded in real ecology, not anecdote.

The long-term goal is a dataset that supports responsible foraging education, regional research, and better understanding of how species respond to landscape and climate patterns.
