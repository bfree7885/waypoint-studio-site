ForageCast

Terrain-aware foraging forecasts.

ForageCast is a future GIS-based foraging prediction tool that combines terrain, weather, seasonality, and ecological patterns to help users understand where wild edible species may be likely to appear.

Subscription model coming soon.

V1 focus

Morel mushrooms

Features

Terrain suitability

Slope, aspect, soil context, and landscape position mapped to species habitat preferences.

Weather and timing signals

Recent precipitation, temperature patterns, and seasonal windows integrated into forecasts.

Species-specific season models

Focused models built around individual species — starting with morels in v1.

Field notes and observations

Structured logging to connect real-world finds with landscape and weather context.

Education-first foraging guidance

Context and ecology over hotspots — built to support learning, not extraction.

Ethics

ForageCast is designed for education, responsible harvest, and ecological awareness. It does not replace expert identification or local regulations.
