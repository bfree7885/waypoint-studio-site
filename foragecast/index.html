        Scouting map

        Local scouting map

        Where to scout — not where to harvest. Zones blend live weather with elevation and slope proxies.

        Not a mushroom map. Broad scouting zones — not property lines, parcel boundaries, or permission to enter land. Use public access maps and local regulations before entering any area.

        Enter a location, then generate the map.

        Using regional elevation estimate.

        High scouting zone Medium zone Low zone
        • Real: Geocoding, 7-day precip/temp (Open-Meteo), per-zone elevation when available.
        • Prototype: Slope/aspect proxy and habitat scoring — not forest cover, ownership, or survey data.
        • Zones: Approximate scouting areas across the visible map — not exact land parcels.

        Ethical foraging rules

        • Know property boundaries before you enter any land.
        • Follow state and local regulations, permits, and seasonal restrictions.
        • Harvest lightly — leave enough for regeneration and wildlife.
        • Leave habitat intact. Avoid trampling understory and disturbing soils.
        • Do not rely on app predictions as permission to enter land.
        • Learn identification from trusted sources before consuming anything.
