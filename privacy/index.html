Privacy

Private by default. Sharing is optional. No social graph.

Our stance

Waypoint Studio builds education-first tools for observing the living world. We do not use likes, followers, streaks, leaderboards, or engagement pressure. Personal notes, saved opportunities, and location use should stay under your control.

What stays in your browser

  • Waypoint Volunteer — saves, lists, completed marks, and personal notes use localStorage on your device. They are not uploaded to Waypoint servers (this static site has none).
  • ForageCast — a small flag may remember that you dismissed the beta banner.
  • We do not operate accounts or shared profiles on this static platform build.

Location

Sheds, ForageCast, and Volunteer may ask for your location only when you choose Locate / “Use my location.” Coordinates are used in the browser to center maps and estimate distances. Denying permission still allows demo-region browsing.

Location is approximate for discovery. Sensitive wildlife or private property details should never be shared publicly from these tools.

Third-party requests

  • Fonts — Google Fonts (when pages load).
  • Maps — Leaflet (CDN) and CARTO / OpenStreetMap tiles.
  • Weather / elevation / geocoding — Open-Meteo public APIs (ForageCast, Volunteer Today context).
  • Forms — ForageCast beta / waitlist / feedback may submit to Formspree when configured.

We do not embed invasive analytics or advertising trackers in this repository.

Photography & scenes

Waypoint Scenes displays curated photography. There is no Photo Coach upload pipeline in this repository build. Do not assume EXIF stripping or local image processing unless a future product page states it clearly.

Demo & illustrative data

Volunteer opportunity catalogs and Sheds map zones may be illustrative demo data. Demo organization links may point to example domains. Treat them as product prototypes, not verified live listings.
