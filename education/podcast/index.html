Waypoint Field Notes

Conversations and reflections about landscapes, ecosystems, making, observation, and practical living.

Episodes listed below are placeholders. No audio yet.

Episode 01 — Reading a Hillside in Winter

Opening observation
Snow reveals where wind slows and where animals might rest out of sight.
Core idea
Terrain is readable. Observation begins before identification.
Examples
Bench lines, lee slopes, edge zones between forest and field.
Questions to take outside
Where does snow linger? Where does it melt first? What might that suggest about use?

Episode 02 — Slow Work at the Bench

Opening observation
A hand plane leaves a surface that machines often skip past.
Core idea
Making useful things slowly builds attention and restraint.
Examples
A stool, a crate, a simple shelf—objects that earn their place.
Questions to take outside
What in your daily life is worth making by hand? What is not?

Episode 03 — Habitat Before Species

Opening observation
A bird seen once is a moment. A habitat understood is a context.
Core idea
Learning place precedes learning names.
Examples
Edge, canopy layer, water proximity, seasonal food sources.
Questions to take outside
What would need to be true for many species to use this place?
