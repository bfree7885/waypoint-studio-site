    About Waypoint Volunteer

    Waypoint Volunteer helps you discover meaningful opportunities to help your community and the natural world. It answers one question: What good can I do today?

    This is not a volunteer management platform, social network, or CRM. There are no points, streaks, leaderboards, followers, or public profiles.

    Saves, interests, and personal lists stay in your browser (local-first). Organizations and opportunities in this build are illustrative demo data for the central Pennsylvania region.
