Sheds

Ethical shed hunting intelligence.

Sheds is a future field tool for responsible shed hunting, terrain understanding, seasonal timing, observation logging, and wildlife-aware decision support.

Subscription model coming soon.

Features

Field View

Map-first interface designed for use on the ground — explore terrain layers in real time.

Terrain Insight

Slope, aspect, terrain position, and habitat context combined into probability-weighted zones.

Seasonal timing

Understand when landscape conditions align with shed activity — not just where.

Observation logging

Each find becomes a structured data point linked to terrain at time of discovery.

Photo-based shed records

Field photos paired with estimated characteristics and landscape context.

Research-grade ecology dataset

Contributions build toward understanding wildlife movement and habitat use over time.

Ethics

During closed seasons, hotspot-style guidance is disabled and the app shifts into education mode. The goal is to reduce wildlife pressure, support responsible off-trail awareness, and help users understand landscapes without harming animals.
