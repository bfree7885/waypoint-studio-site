Waypoint Studio is a long-term creative portfolio and archive.

Its purpose is to collect and share projects, education, photography, maps, making, art, experiments, and field observations.

Some sections document ideas. Some teach. Some collect. Some simply exist because they were enjoyable to make.

This site is not optimized for growth. It exists to document making and curiosity over time. Nothing here is intended to be finished.
