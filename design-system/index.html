Aurora Design System

A National Park visitor center designed by photographers.

Premium, quiet, natural, modern, elegant — inspired by northern lights, New England autumn, aspen and evergreen forests, golden hour, blue hour, and night photography. Not SaaS chrome. Not flashy.
